Dilli Dhakka Express

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. three.min.js (Three.js r128, MIT licence) is included, so it runs from the folder.
Fonts (Bungee, Hind) load from Google Fonts when online and fall back to system fonts offline.

Controls: W/S gas and brake, A/D or arrows steer, E hold to fiddle the meter, Space horn, M mute, Esc exit.
