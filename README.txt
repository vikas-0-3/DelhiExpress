Dilli Dhakka Express

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. three.min.js (Three.js r128, MIT licence) is included, so it runs from the folder.
Fonts (Bungee, Hind) load from Google Fonts when online and fall back to system fonts offline.

Controls: W/S gas and brake, A/D or arrows steer, E hold to fiddle the meter, Space horn, M mute, Esc exit.
The HUD shows the onboard passenger count (0-2) and updates on pickup and drop-off.
Race CPU driver Bhola: he independently steers between lanes, avoids traffic, seeks coins,
and picks up and drops off his own passengers. Complete rides and collect bonuses to beat
his earnings before the shift timer ends.

Road hazards include fallen cyclists, dogs that chase you and flee after a bump, and
occasional puddles. Puddles splash nearby pedestrians, who show a Hindi complaint bubble.
There is no system text-to-speech. The engine, horn, dog bark, crash, pickup, coin, fare,
and splash sounds are synthesized locally with Web Audio; press M to mute. No sound files
are downloaded. Momos stalls appear along the footpaths.
