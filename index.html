दिल्ली धक्का एक्सप्रेस

Dhuaan bhi, jam bhi, meter bhi. 3D e-rickshaw chaos from Lal Qila to the Ring Road, AQI 400+.

Rickshaw ka rang
  • Drive: W gas, S brake/reverse, A D steer, Shift nitro.
  • Sawari: roll slowly past a waving passenger to pick up, stop slowly in the green bay to drop.
  • Meter: hold E to run it fast. Fare climbs, gussa fills. At 100 they walk off.
  • Grab: marigold coins pay up to ₹25 with combo streaks. Chai cup = +8 seconds. Every 3 completed rides earns a ₹75 mission bonus.
  • Challenge: complete rides, chain coin pickups, dodge traffic and build your best single-player score.
  • Power-up: hold Shift or NITRO for a speed burst. It recharges while cruising. Near-misses and coin streaks boost your score.
  • Voice: turn on the speaker to hear Raju's dramatic Dilli reactions when things go wrong.
  • Road: puddles splash nearby pedestrians. Horn: Space. Cows will not care. Mute: M
